# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-05

First public version.

- The Grounded Commits commit-message format: an `area: outcome` subject, a
  problem-first body, and a trailer block carrying the evidence, with
  Conventional Commits available as an optional typed header.
- The pull-request body for branches that land by rebase and fast-forward: a
  short review brief that points at the commits instead of repeating them.
- Landing by rebase and fast-forward, including how to do it on GitHub.
- The research behind both, with every claim cited to its primary source.
